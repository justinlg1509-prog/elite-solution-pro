What hard inquiries are

Every time a lender pulls your credit for an application, a hard inquiry is recorded. Many in a short time can lower your score. Only apply for what you need.

Paying rent can help you

If your rent payments are reported to the credit bureaus, you build history and show good payment habits every month.

Your mortgage doesn't have to last 30 years

Extra payments toward principal shorten the term and save you thousands in interest. Ask your lender to apply them to principal only.

Use less than 30% of your limit

How much of your cards you use weighs heavily on your score. Keeping balances low is one of the fastest ways to improve it.
