Business tips

What we share every week on Instagram, in short.

Separate your money

A business bank account just for the company keeps your numbers clear, protects you and makes taxes much easier.

LLC or sole proprietor?

An LLC separates your personal assets from the business. Choosing the right structure from the start saves you problems later.

Know your numbers every month

Income, expenses and margin. If you check them every month, you make decisions with data instead of guesses.

Automate what you repeat

Quotes, reminders, follow-ups and reports. If you do it every week, a system or an AI tool can probably do it for you.
