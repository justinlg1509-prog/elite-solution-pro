Summary

  • The Website has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies.
  • The contact form does not send your data to our servers. It prepares a WhatsApp message on your device, and you decide whether to send it.
  • We use the information you send us only to answer you and to provide the services you ask for.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or Instagram, or when you send the message prepared by our contact form, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, the stage of your business and the content of your message.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information through the Website, WhatsApp or email. If a service requires documents, we will tell you how to share them securely.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data needed to deliver and protect the Website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested, the referring page and the date and time of the request. We use this data only for security, troubleshooting and keeping the Website running.

Information stored on your device

If you switch languages, the Website saves your choice (English or Spanish) in your browser's local storage under the name esp-lang. It contains no personal information, is not sent to us and you can delete it by clearing your browser data. We do not use cookies.

Interactive tools

The self-check, the example diagnostic and the savings calculator run entirely in your browser. What you enter in them is not sent to us or stored.

2. How we use information

  • To answer your questions and schedule and hold your consultation.
  • To provide, manage and follow up on the services you request.
  • To keep records of our communications and our work.
  • To protect the Website and our business, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix errors.
  • To comply with the law, respond to lawful requests and enforce our agreements.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Service providers that help us run the Website and our business, such as website hosting, email and messaging (including WhatsApp, operated by Meta), and business software. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Third parties you ask us to work with, such as a government agency, bank or other provider, only with your direction or permission.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our clients, our business or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale or reorganization of our business, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or for profiling.

4. Third-party services

The Website contains links to WhatsApp, Instagram and email. When you use them, the information you share is also handled by those companies under their own privacy policies (for example, WhatsApp and Instagram). We are not responsible for their practices.

Our fonts and images are served from our own domain, so loading the Website does not send your information to font or image providers.

5. Phone calls, text and WhatsApp messages

We contact you only to answer requests you start. We do not send marketing text messages or make marketing calls using automated technology or prerecorded voices without your prior express written consent.

If we ever offer promotional messages, we will ask for your consent separately. Giving consent will never be a condition of buying any service, message and data rates may apply, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP or by telling us. We do not share your mobile number or messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy: to answer you, to provide our services, and to meet legal, tax, accounting and recordkeeping obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on the entire Website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the state where you live (for example, California, New Jersey and other states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • Know whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete personal information you provided or that we hold about you.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling (we do not do any of these).
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

We offer these rights to every visitor in the United States, wherever you live. To make a request, email info@elitesolutionpro.com or message us at 848-900-9621. We will verify your request using information we already have, and we will respond within 45 days (we may extend this once by another 45 days if necessary, and we will tell you why). You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that the agent is authorized.

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying with the word "Appeal". We will answer your appeal within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state Attorney General.

9. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track visitors across websites, so the Website works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

10. Children

The Website and our services are intended for adults and businesses. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Notice to California residents

In the last 12 months we have collected the categories of personal information described in Section 1 (identifiers and contact details, the content of your messages, and internet activity such as technical log data), from you and from your device, for the purposes described in Section 2. We disclosed them for business purposes only to the recipients described in Section 3. We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would require offering a right to limit. Under California Civil Code Section 1798.83, California residents may also ask us about disclosures for direct marketing; we make none.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If the changes are significant, we will post a clear notice on the Website before they take effect.
