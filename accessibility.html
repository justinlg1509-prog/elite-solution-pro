Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Elite Solution Pro wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and contact us easily.

Our goal

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with visual, hearing, motor and cognitive disabilities.

What we do

  • Clear page structure with headings, landmarks and a "Skip to content" link.
  • The whole website can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text and controls with sufficient color contrast.
  • Form fields with visible labels and error messages announced to screen readers.
  • Animations are turned off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • The full website is available in English and Spanish.
  • No time limits, and no content that flashes.
  • We review the website when we make changes and test it with automated tools and by hand.

Known limitations

Some parts of the experience depend on third-party services, such as WhatsApp and Instagram, whose accessibility we do not control. If any part of our website does not work for you, we will give you the same information or service in another way, for example by phone or email.

Feedback and help

If you find a barrier on our website or need information in a different format, please tell us. Include the page and a short description of the problem, and we will do our best to fix it and respond within 5 business days.
